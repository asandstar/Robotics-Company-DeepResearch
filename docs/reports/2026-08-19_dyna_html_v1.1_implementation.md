## Outcome

Dyna.html v1.1 restructures the technical core around all five works listed in Dyna's official Research Index. Each work is now a first-class research dossier rather than an item that exists only in the expandable timeline. Dyna-2 remains longer because its official technical report discloses substantially more experimental detail; parity means consistent audit-question coverage, not manufactured symmetry or inferred facts.

## Five-study sequence

1. **Dynamism v1 / DYNA-1** — long-horizon reliability, foundation reward model, progress estimation, recovery and deployment feedback.
2. **Open-World Dexterity / DYNA-1i** — selected environment generalization, office post-training, the seen row, the single combined unseen row and public demonstrations.
3. **DYNA-1 Pre-Training** — selected zero-shot behaviors and low-data robot adaptation, with a dedicated protocol-disclosure boundary.
4. **Dyna-2 Scaling Study** — human-video scaling, WAM, three evidence stages, cross-embodiment prediction and matched robot post-training.
5. **Dyna-2 Infrastructure** — episode storage, ingestion, curation, manifests, delivery, distributed optimization and failure recovery.

Every dossier covers the same nine questions: research question, relationship to the preceding work, data, model/mechanism, evaluation protocol, reported results, directly supported interpretation, not-established interpretation and remaining bottleneck.

## Evidence safeguards

- DYNA-1's six-week progression is labeled company-reported progression rather than a controlled learning curve; checkpoint and configuration comparability remain unknown.
- DYNA-1i's unseen result is a **single reported 30-minute row labeled “Lobby / Parking / CoRL”**. It is not rewritten as three separate 30-minute tests.
- The official-index date `2025-10-15` and current article-page label `December 2025` are retained as a source metadata discrepancy. It was editorially rechecked on 2026-08-19 without changing the research cutoff or speculating about the cause.
- DYNA-1 Pre-Training separates selected zero-shot evidence from adaptation evidence and retains unknown task mapping, attempt denominator, success rubric, accepted-versus-collected data, reset/setup/operator time and contamination controls.
- Dyna-2's 39-task result remains an offline robot-action prediction evaluation, not physical zero-shot control.
- Dyna-2's 14-task physical suite remains explicitly dependent on task-specific robot post-training.
- `20 → 28 → 45 → 53` remains a mean normalized score over heterogeneous task metrics, not a percentage success rate.
- The deployment registry's old `20/28/45/53%` field was corrected to `20/28/45/53 ... (not percentages)`; the embedded runtime view applies the same correction.
- Future-video prediction remains a training objective for the disclosed scaling policy, not an inference-time imagined-video planning requirement.
- Dyna-2 attribution remains bounded to the disclosed mid-level dexterity role; integrated planning, navigation, control, recovery and safety are not automatically attributed to Dyna-2.
- Infrastructure improvements remain internal, workload-specific, and attributed to the joint redesign rather than a single tool.
- Unknowns remain visible and were not completed with inference.
